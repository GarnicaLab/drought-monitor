Columbia Basin drought tracking

Weekly U.S. Drought Monitor conditions for the Washington counties where potatoes are grown. Figures rebuild automatically every Thursday.

How to read these. The U.S. Drought Monitor reports the percent of land area in each category, not the percent of potato acreage. Columbia Basin potatoes are irrigated, so drought category here reflects water supply and allocation risk rather than water stress in a managed field.

Data: U.S. Drought Monitor, a joint product of the National Drought Mitigation Center at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, the U.S. Department of Agriculture, and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

Figures and code: Garnica Lab, Washington State University. Raw weekly records are in the repository as CSV.
