Columbia Basin Drought Tracking

Weekly U.S. Drought Monitor conditions for the Washington counties where potatoes are grown. Rebuilt automatically every Thursday.

How to read these. The U.S. Drought Monitor is released each Thursday and reflects conditions through the preceding Tuesday. It reports the percent of land area in each category, not the percent of potato acreage. Columbia Basin potatoes are irrigated, so drought category here reflects water supply and allocation risk rather than water stress in a managed field.

Method

The U.S. Drought Monitor publishes national, state, and county statistics. The figures above aggregate the county records for Adams, Benton, Franklin, Grant, Walla Walla, and Yakima counties, which account for most of Washington's potato production. Areas are summed in square miles and then expressed as a percentage, so no weighting assumptions are involved.
